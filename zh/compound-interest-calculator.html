数学算法模型与常见问题

What formula governs compound interest with deposits?

Future Value = P*(1 + r/n)^(nt) + PMT * [((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n)].

How often is interest compounded in this model?

This simulator compounds interest on a standard monthly frequency (12 times per year) matching periodic additions.

Does this simulator account for inflation?

It calculates nominal future value; real return can be approximated by subtracting expected inflation from the rate.
