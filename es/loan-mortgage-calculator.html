Modelos Matemáticos y Preguntas Frecuentes

What amortization formula is used?

The standard monthly payment formula is: M = P * [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1].

Why does total repayment exceed borrowed principal?

The surplus consists of cumulative interest paid to the lender over the lifetime of the financing term.

Can this calculate auto loans and student debt?

Yes, any fixed-rate amortizing installment loan adheres to this mathematical formula.
