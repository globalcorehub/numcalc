Modelos Financeiros e Dúvidas Frequentes

How is the simplified ratio determined?

Using the Euclidean Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) algorithm to find the lowest integer ratio.

Does changing W2 automatically update H2?

Yes, modifying width or height automatically computes the reciprocal dimension while maintaining geometry.

What are common video and monitor aspect ratios?

16:9 (1920x1080 Full HD, 3840x2160 4K UHD), 21:9 (Ultrawide), and 4:3 (Legacy).
